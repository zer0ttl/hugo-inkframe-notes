Constraints shape us. They force decisions, surface priorities, and remove the noise. Without them, it's easy to drift into bloated ideas and endless revisions.

Some of the most iconic work exists because of constraints—whether it was a tiny screen, a tight budget, or just a hard deadline.

When I start a project, I try to define the right constraints early. Not too many. Not too few. Just enough to create focus and invite creativity.

> Constraints aren’t the opposite of freedom. They’re the foundation for it.

The goal isn’t to remove constraints. It's to work with them intentionally—so they lead to clarity, not frustration.
